Imaging, audio and video

Medical and clinical images, facial photographs and 3D scans, and audio and video recordings. Faces and voices are biometric identifiers, which decides where these files can be used.

Images

An ImagingFile is a medical or clinical image, such as a DICOM CT or MRI series, a microscopy image or a clinical photograph. Every image states the technique that produced it in measurementTechnique; analysis depends on it. Values map to OBI assay terms where OBI has one, and to NCIT or SNOMED CT terms where it does not.

Facial images and 3D scans

An image or 3D surface model of a person's face, such as those taken for dysmorphology assessment or facial phenotyping, is a FacialImagingFile. Its measurementTechnique says how it was captured: a photograph, a 3D surface scan, or a CT or MRI. 2D photographs and 3D models go to different facial analysis tools.

A 3D model is an OBJ, STL, glTF or PLY file. An OBJ model's material file is a separate FacialImagingFile with media type model/mtl. PLY has no registered media type, so a PLY model is application/octet-stream.

A face is a biometric identifier, so FacialImagingFile is a biometric class: every file counts as containing biometric identifiers and stays in the trusted research environment (TRE). The file's sensitivity can still set the other flags, as the photograph above does for dates, but not containsBiometricIdentifiers.

Audio and video

AudioFile and VideoFile accept any audio or video media type respectively, such as audio/wav or video/mp4.

Whether a recording identifies a person depends on what it records, so these classes are not biometric. A recording of a person's voice, or a video that shows a face, contains biometric identifiers: set sensitivity.containsBiometricIdentifiers to true on the file, as the voice recording above does. Files with biometric identifiers stay in the TRE.

In a sidecar

Images and recordings are grouped in a FileCollection file set. An acquisition table describing them is a DocumentationFile. The images and video recipe shows a facial photograph, a gait video and an acquisition table in one batch.
