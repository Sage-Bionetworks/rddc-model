Imaging, audio and video

Medical and clinical images, facial photographs and 3D scans, and audio and video recordings. Faces and voices are biometric identifiers, which determine where these files can be used.

Images

A medical or clinical image, such as a CT or MRI series, a microscopy image or a clinical photograph, is a DicomImageFile when it is DICOM and an ImageFile otherwise: TIFF or OME-TIFF, PNG, JPEG, JPEG 2000, or a whole-slide or NIfTI format with no registered media type. DICOM headers carry patient details that are de-identified in place; other formats carry none, though burned-in text counts as free text. Every image requires measurementTechnique for analysis. Values map to OBI assay terms where OBI has one, and to NCIT or SNOMED CT terms where it does not.

Facial images and 3D scans

An image or 3D surface model of a person's face, such as those taken for dysmorphology assessment or facial phenotyping, is a FacialImagingFile. Its measurementTechnique says how it was captured: a photograph, a 3D surface scan, or a CT or MRI. 2D photographs and 3D models go to different facial analysis tools. It is not a subclass of the image classes: rules for images do not apply to it.

A 3D model is an OBJ, STL, glTF or PLY file. An OBJ model's material file is a separate FacialImagingFile with media type model/mtl. PLY has no registered media type, so a PLY model is application/octet-stream.

A face is a biometric identifier, so FacialImagingFile is a biometric class: every file counts as containing biometric identifiers and never leaves INTAKE. The file's sensitivity can still set the other flags, as the photograph above does for dates, but not containsBiometricIdentifiers.

Audio and video

AudioFile and VideoFile accept any audio or video media type respectively, such as audio/wav or video/mp4.

Whether a recording identifies a person depends on its content, so these classes are not biometric. A recording of a person's voice, or a video that shows a face, contains biometric identifiers: set sensitivity.containsBiometricIdentifiers to true on the file, as the voice recording above does. Files with biometric identifiers never leave INTAKE. Speech and on-screen text count as free text; recordings carry no address, so containsZipCodes is false unless set.

Acquisition details

A table describing how images or recordings were acquired, such as the device, settings or date of each, is a DocumentationFile that points to the files it describes.
